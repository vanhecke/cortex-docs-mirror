# Investigate child tenant data

With Cortex XSIAM multi-tenancy, you can investigate the Cortex XSIAM child tenant data.

By default, Cortex XSIAM displays data for your tenant. To display data for your child tenant, select the tenant from the drop-down.

![mssp-select-tenant.png](https://2786854933-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FAEIjuYE3RXcIfmuQnBbm%2Fuploads%2Fgit-blob-ca729e995ff67f164b127280920d5be25b78ecdf%2F9f505187a1447e3575a34103804992dc21a02250f80140d4965d2ea3ffcf7af0.png?alt=media)

Some common tasks that you might perform include:

* Investigate cases on a child tenant.
* Investigate issues on a child tenant.
