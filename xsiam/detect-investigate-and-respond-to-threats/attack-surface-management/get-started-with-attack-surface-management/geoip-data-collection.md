# GeoIP data collection

Attack Surface Management (ASM) in Cortex XSIAM geoIP data collection enables you to confirm that your actual network distribution is consistent with what you believe your global footprint to be. GeoIP data is especially important for security organizations to identify compliance violations, such as data residing in restricted locations, and to drive efficient remediations where customers leverage geoIP data to determine infrastructure location, who owns the asset, and where to route notifications.
