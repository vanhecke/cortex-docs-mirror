# Resolve the case

After analyzing a case, you can start remediation in the Cortex XSIAM **Resolution Center**. This process involves executing specific tasks to address the problems identified in the case. Once the remediation tasks are completed and verified, you can officially close the case to reflect its updated status and maintain an accurate audit trail.
