# Deleted content

The **Trash** folder acts as a safety net for your workspace, enabling the safe deletion and recovery of all deleted dashboards, reports, and widgets. From this folder, you can restore items or permanently delete them. You can access the **Trash** from the ![](https://2786854933-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FAEIjuYE3RXcIfmuQnBbm%2Fuploads%2Fx1GKN6Nk5W3S0Q4QhBb9%2FUnknown%20image?alt=media\&token=786b200a-6bb0-4ab0-be97-c1ab98df83c3)three dots menu on the **Dashboard Manager** and **Reports** page.

### Retention Policy

When you delete a dashboard, report, or widget, it is not immediately lost:

* Deleted items are automatically moved to the **Trash** folder.
* Items remain in the **Trash** for 30 days.
* After 30 days, items are permanently deleted from the tenant and cannot be recovered.
