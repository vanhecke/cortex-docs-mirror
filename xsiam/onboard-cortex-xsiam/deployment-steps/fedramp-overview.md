# FedRAMP overview

The Federal Risk and Authorization Management Program (FedRAMP) provides a standardized approach to security assessment, authorization, and continuous monitoring for cloud products and services used by the U.S. government. This program ensures that federal information remains secure while allowing agencies to adopt cloud solutions efficiently.
